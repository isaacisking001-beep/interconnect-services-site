Refund eligibility depends on the type of service purchased and whether work has already started or been completed.

2. Cancellation Requests

Customers may request cancellation by contacting Interconnect Services LLC as soon as possible.

If a cancellation request is received before work or service delivery has begun, we may provide a full or partial refund, depending on the circumstances and any applicable third-party charges.

If work has already begun, the amount refunded may be reduced to account for services already performed, materials purchased, administrative costs, or other non-recoverable expenses.

3. Completed Services

Once a service has been fully delivered, completed, installed, configured, or otherwise provided as agreed, it is generally not eligible for a refund.

If you believe a service was not delivered according to the agreed scope of work, please contact us so we can review the matter and, where appropriate, provide a correction or other reasonable resolution.

4. Subscriptions and Recurring Services

For recurring services or subscriptions, customers may request cancellation before the next billing cycle.

Cancellation generally prevents future charges but does not automatically result in a refund for a previous billing period.

Where applicable, refunds for unused portions of a subscription will be considered on a case-by-case basis.
